Rikiki is the trick-taking game you pull out after dinner. Everyone on their own phone, around the same table — or far apart.

HOW IT WORKS
• Create a game, share the 4-letter code by WhatsApp or SMS
• Each round, call how many tricks you think you'll win
• The whole point is to be exactly right: no more, no less

WHAT'S INSIDE
• 3 to 8 players, online, in real time
• Bots to fill the table when you're short
• Three formats: blitz (10 min), classic (30 min) or climb
• Asynchronous mode: everyone plays when they can, over days
• Three scoring systems: find the one your family plays
• The hook rule: the calls can never add up to the tricks available
• Friend groups with standings across games
• Game history and personal statistics
• A notification when it's your turn
• 24 languages, picked automatically from your phone
• Colourblind-friendly four-colour deck

WITHOUT THE USUAL NONSENSE
• One-time purchase, no subscription
• No ads
• No in-app purchases
• No account required: a nickname is enough to play
• Data hosted in the European Union

An optional email address restores your progress on another device — no password to remember.
