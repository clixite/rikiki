First release of Rikiki.

A trick-taking game for 3 to 8 players, everyone on their own phone. Create a game, share the four-letter code, and play — around the same table or miles apart.

• Three formats: blitz, classic or climb
• “At your own pace” mode: everyone plays when they can
• Three scoring systems
• Automatic players when you are short of people
• No ads, no in-app purchases, no subscription
